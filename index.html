About

Kerala roots, club-focused sound

Julix is a UK-based DJ, producer, drummer and content creator bringing a distinctive South Indian identity to electronic music.

Her sets blend Techno, House and EDM with the rhythms, melodies and energy of her Kerala roots, creating a sound that is both club-focused and culturally unique.

From clubs across the UK and Ireland to destinations, resorts and cafés, she brings an energetic, immersive approach to every performance.

For me, DJing is about connection — reading the room, building tension, and letting it all go at just the right moment. Julix
